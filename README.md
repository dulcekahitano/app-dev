# Naruto (2002)

## A. Introduction

### Title

*Naruto*

### Description

Naruto is a Japanese anime series about a young ninja named Naruto Uzumaki who dreams of becoming the Hokage, the strongest and most respected ninja leader of his village. Despite being ignored and disliked by many people, he never gives up on his dream. Throughout the series, he faces difficult challenges, makes new friends, and learns important lessons about friendship, loyalty, and never giving up.

### Genre

- Action
- Adventure
- Fantasy
- Comedy
- Martial Arts

### Release Year

*2002*

---

## B. Synopsis

The story follows Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja who lives in the Hidden Leaf Village. When he was just a baby, the Nine-Tailed Fox was sealed inside him, causing many villagers to fear and avoid him. Because of this, Naruto grows up feeling lonely and dreams of becoming the Hokage so that everyone will recognize and respect him.

After graduating from the Ninja Academy, Naruto becomes part of Team 7 alongside Sasuke Uchiha and Sakura Haruno, under the guidance of their teacher, Kakashi Hatake. Together, they complete missions, face powerful enemies, and learn how to work as a team. As the story progresses, Naruto develops strong friendships while also facing challenges involving his rival, Sasuke, who eventually leaves the village in search of greater power. Naruto promises to bring Sasuke back and continues training to become stronger. His journey teaches him the importance of friendship, determination, sacrifice, and believing in himself.

---

## C. Characters

Here are five of the most important characters in the anime:

- *Naruto Uzumaki* – The main protagonist of the series. He is energetic, determined, and dreams of becoming the Hokage.
- *Sasuke Uchiha* – Naruto's rival and teammate. He is talented and serious, and he seeks strength to accomplish his personal goals.
- *Sakura Haruno* – A member of Team 7 who is intelligent and determined to improve her abilities as a ninja.
- *Kakashi Hatake* – The leader and teacher of Team 7. He is a skilled ninja known for his calm personality and intelligence.
- *Hinata Hyuga* – A shy but kind-hearted ninja who admires Naruto and gradually becomes more confident.

## D. Personal Review

### Why I Chose This Anime

I chose Naruto because it is an interesting anime with an enjoyable story and memorable characters. I like how Naruto starts as an underestimated young ninja but continues working hard to achieve his dream. The series also shows the importance of friendship, determination, and never giving up even when life becomes difficult.

### Three Things I Liked About the Anime

1. *The Characters* – I like how each character has their own personality, background, and goals. Their relationships make the story more interesting.
2. *The Story* – I enjoyed how the story develops over time, especially Naruto's journey from an ordinary ninja student to a stronger and more confident person.
3. *The Action Scenes* – The ninja battles, special abilities, and different fighting techniques make the anime entertaining and exciting to watch.

### My Rating

*9/10*

I would give this anime a 9 out of 10 because of its interesting storyline, memorable characters, and exciting battles. It also teaches valuable lessons about friendship, hard work, and determination. Although some episodes can feel slow, I still think it is a great anime.

### Recommendation

I recommend Naruto to people who enjoy action, adventure, and fantasy anime. It is a great series for viewers who enjoy stories about personal growth, friendship, rivalry, and achieving dreams. I especially recommend it to people who want to watch an anime with an engaging story and characters that develop throughout the series.

Naruto teaches us that no matter how difficult the journey becomes, we should never give up on our dreams.
